ABOUT THE ATLAS

A selective history of major Christian traditions and events, told from an Eastern Orthodox perspective. The early councils and the Great Schism are presented through the Orthodox Church’s understanding of its continuity with the early Church. Branches show broad historical relationships. Dotted lines mark movements with multiple roots or connections through earlier reform movements. Names for early churches often reflect later usage.

Council coverage. The Great Orthodox Council markers include every later council named in the 2016 Crete encyclical and the Council of Crete itself, alongside selected inter-Orthodox councils on church governance. The designation groups councils by wider significance. Their participation, reception and standing vary. The Quinisext and the 1347 Hesychast synod appear alongside them as related councils.
